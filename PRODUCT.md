# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, Lucide — explicitly preferred in the supplied brief.

## Users
Value-conscious shoppers deciding among unfamiliar products; workshop participants evaluating a BuildYYC startup prototype.

## Product Purpose
Surface overlooked purchase requirements before a shopper commits. Success is a demonstrable discovery → reversible elimination → transparent weighted comparison journey.

## Positioning
Questions are chosen from differences among the actual shortlist. Hard blockers and scoring priorities remain independently editable, and eliminated products remain inspectable.

## Operating Context
Local, desktop and mobile browser demo. Two curated purchase scenarios; no live inventory or automated reasoning. The supplied detailed brief authorizes autonomous implementation choices without a separate interview.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Pure deterministic decision engine, typed mock data, estimated acquisition costs, explicitly unknown information, checked direct evidence, and local browser persistence. No backend, authentication, payment, real LLM, scraping, deployment, or CI.

## Evidence on Hand
`docs/BRIEF.md` is the supplied product specification. Checked manufacturer pages for Roland FP-10, Roland FP-30X and Kawai ES120 support narrowly scoped claims. Prices, bundles, returns, subjective scores, and couch catalogue are illustrative. No genuine customer reviews or review consensus supplied.

## Product Principles
- Expose uncertainty rather than invent compliance or confidence.
- Preserve every discovered option and explain every exclusion.
- Ask useful questions before showing extensive controls.
- Let users inspect and change what drives the ranking.

## Open decisions
Considered is a provisional product name selected for this MVP. Currency is illustrative CAD; taxes and live availability are outside scope. Robot sensors are deferred pending validation of the first two scenarios.
